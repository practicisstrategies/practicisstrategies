FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need to commit to a package upfront?

Not at all. Our hourly Consultation option lets you start with a single session. If you're considering a package, we always begin with a free discovery call so you can make an informed decision.

How long does the Startup Foundations package take?

Typically 3–5 weeks from kickoff to delivery, depending on feedback cycles. We work efficiently and keep timelines tight so you can launch with momentum.

Can I bundle multiple packages?

Yes — we offer bundled pricing for clients who book multiple services at once. Reach out and we'll put together a custom proposal tailored to your needs.

Do you work with clients outside of Montréal?

Absolutely. We work with clients across Canada and internationally. All engagements can be conducted fully remotely, or in-person when logistics allow.

What happens after the audit deliverable?

You'll receive a full written report with prioritized recommendations. We also offer optional implementation support — whether that's a follow-up consultation or a separate engagement to execute the roadmap.

What industries do you work with?

We've served 12+ industries including tech, non-profit, government, real estate, professional services, and hospitality. Our methodology is sector-agnostic — we adapt to your context.

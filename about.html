Our Story

Why we built Practicis.

Practicis Strategies was founded with one conviction: that good consulting should leave organizations stronger — not just with a report, but with a real shift in how they think, decide, and act.

We saw too many engagements that produced polished decks and little else. So we built a different kind of firm — one that combines strategic rigor with genuine accountability for outcomes.

Today, we partner with founders, executives, and growing teams across industries to deliver the clarity and direction they need to perform at their best — whether that means developing a 3-year strategy, redesigning how a team operates, or orchestrating a high-stakes event that brings the right people together.

Montréal-based. Canada-wide. Remote-ready.

Our Approach

What we stand for.

01

Strategic Clarity

We cut through noise and competing priorities to focus on what actually moves the needle. Every mandate begins with a clear understanding of the problem — and doesn't end until the solution is just as clear.

02

Practical Execution

Our recommendations come with a concrete, actionable roadmap. We don't hand off strategy decks and disappear — we stay close until the work is real, implemented, and producing results.

03

Lasting Impact

We build frameworks, processes, and capabilities that outlast the engagement. The goal is always to leave the organization more capable than we found it — not dependent on external support.
